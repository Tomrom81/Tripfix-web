How it works

From booking email to boarding gate

1

Share it with Tripfix

Got a PDF ticket, a photo of a paper voucher or a booking link? Tap Share and pick Tripfix. No copying, no typing.

2

Tripfix reads the details

Route, date, time, seat, booking code and the QR code. Outbound and return trips become separate bookings, each with what, when, where and who.

3

Your day plan builds itself

Every booking lands on the right day. Add sights, transfers and taxi rides, and every place shows up on the map.

4

Ready at the gate

Open any code full screen at full brightness, so scanners read it first time. Passports and ID cards sit in a locked wallet right next to your tickets.

Privacy

Your trips stay on your phone

Tripfix reads your tickets on the device itself. Bookings, codes and documents are never uploaded to a server or sent to an AI service.

  • No account needed. Open the app and start planning.
  • No cloud, no AI service. Text and codes are recognised on your phone.
  • Works offline. Tickets and saved copies are there without signal.
  • Encrypted document wallet. Passports and ID cards are stored with AES-256 encryption. The key is kept in the Android Keystore and never leaves your phone.
  • Out of cloud backups. Wallet documents are left out of Android and Google backups. To move them, you create a password-protected file yourself.
  • No screenshots. While the wallet is open, the screen cannot be captured or previewed in the app switcher.
  • Only place names leave the phone, to find map locations through OpenStreetMap.

Questions

Which tickets does Tripfix recognise?

Train, flight, bus and ferry tickets, boarding passes, hotel bookings and tickets for tours and events. It works best with tickets in English. If one is not recognised, fill in the booking by hand and send us the ticket so we can teach Tripfix to read it.

Which cities have a template?

Rome is ready now. More of Europe's big cities follow step by step. Each template covers 2 to 5 days of sights. Flights, transfers and restaurants are up to you.

Is there an iPhone version?

Tripfix starts on Android. An iPhone version depends on how the Android app is received.

What does it cost?

Tripfix is free during early access. [Pricing after launch to be decided]

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. There is no Tripfix account and no Tripfix server. To move to a new phone, export your trips to a file and import it there. Wallet documents move separately, in a file locked with a password you choose.

How are my passports and ID cards protected?

Every picture and detail in the wallet is encrypted with AES-256 before it is saved. The key is created on your phone, kept in the Android Keystore and never leaves the device. Pictures are decrypted only in memory while you look at them, and the wallet opens with your fingerprint or PIN. If you uninstall Tripfix the key is gone too, so make a password-protected backup first.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Trips, tickets and codes are all available offline. Reading a booking link or finding a new place on the map needs a connection.
