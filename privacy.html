This policy covers the website tripfix.net and the Tripfix app for Android. Tripfix is built so that your travel data stays on your phone.

The website

  • Hosting. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you open a page, the server receives technical data such as your IP address, the time and the page requested. GitHub uses this data to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • No cookies, no tracking. The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tools.
  • Fonts. All fonts are loaded from tripfix.net itself. No requests go to Google or other font services.
  • Email. If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply. We delete them when they are no longer needed.

The app

  • No account, no Tripfix server. Trips, bookings, tickets, plans and wallet documents are stored only on your phone. We cannot see them.
  • Reading tickets. Text and codes in PDFs, photos and images are recognised on your phone. Nothing is sent to an AI service.
  • Text recognition and document scanner. For photos the app uses Google ML Kit and the Google document scanner, both through Google Play services on your phone. The recognition runs on the device. Google Play services may send technical usage and diagnostic data to Google, as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • Map locations. To find the position of a place, the app sends the place name or address to the OpenStreetMap search service Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation, UK). The service also receives your IP address. Nothing else from your trip is sent.
  • Links you share. When you share a booking link with Tripfix, the app opens that web page to read it. The website you linked receives your IP address, as with any visit.
  • Wallet. Passports, ID cards and other documents are encrypted with AES-256 on your phone. The key stays in the Android Keystore. Wallet documents are excluded from Android and Google backups.
  • Export. Export files are created only when you ask for them. Where you save or send them is your choice.
  • Permissions. The camera is used only when you scan a document. Biometric unlock (fingerprint) is handled by Android; the app never receives your fingerprint.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Write to hello@tripfix.net. Data stored in the app you can delete yourself at any time by deleting trips or uninstalling the app.

You can also complain to the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde), Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, www.dsb.gv.at.

Changes

If the app or website changes how data is handled, we update this page and the date at the top.
